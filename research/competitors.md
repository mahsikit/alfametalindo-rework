## What Makes Alfa Metalindo Different

Most of the above are general welding distributors — they stock mainly mild-steel and standard stainless products for fabrication. Alfa Metalindo's differentiation:

1. **Exotic-alloy focus**: Duplex, Super Duplex, Inconel/Nickel-based, Titanium, CRA alloys — the technically demanding, high-margin end of the market. Most general distributors don't stock these.

2. **Metrode UK distribution**: Metrode is the premium energy-market consumable line (refineries, LNG, fertilizer). Most competitors do not carry Metrode.

3. **Service beyond products**: Welding habitat rental (WorkSafe), PWHT machines (Alfa PWHT), pipe cold-cut & bevel (Tri Tool) — turnkey hot-work solutions that box-shifters don't provide.

4. **Oil & gas track record**: 23 years supplying named majors (Pertamina, Total, BP, ConocoPhillips, etc.) — most B2B competitors don't have this published track record.

5. **Authorized distributor network**: 13 global brand relationships — breadth that most single-brand distributors can't match.
